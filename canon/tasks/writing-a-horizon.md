# Writing a horizon

A horizon is what is worked toward now, over roughly one to two months. It is not a standing aim or one per area; do not invent one for an inactive part of the app. It takes several programmes. A deliverable with one end and one programme is a programme, not a horizon. Split wording too large for the period before confirmation.

Before drafting, read the area pages, archived programmes of the relevant part, open ends on record and the app (with `mc shot`). Distinguish facts actually read from unavailable evidence; do not present an assumption as an observation. The enduring direction belongs to the area, not the horizon.

Before confirmation, the desk writes `basis.md` beside `playback.md`: what runs today, binding decisions, numbered gaps with where each was read, and what is not decided. Playback prerequisites name basis gaps by number. Anything not yet designed (a new thing or “polished”) goes under not decided: a programme document settles it, never the cut into goals. The basis is authored context, not parsed. Verification that follows the orders (by Martin, on a Mac or on a device) is listed in the basis as information, under the gap it belongs to; no programme carries it (Martin, 2026-11-21).

Draft English playback with exactly these five non-empty headings in order:

## How it is read

Describe the state experienced by the user at the end of the period, not the
area's enduring direction.

## How one sees it is reached

Describe the final judgement, which may include Martin's own use and
satisfaction. Separately list prerequisites readable by the manager from code
and standards at origin/main, area pages or app observations with `mc shot`.
Production use, unmeasured elapsed time and satisfaction are not such facts.
Report observed prerequisites and outstanding personal judgement separately;
never infer the latter from the former.

Every requirement and prerequisite states its reason: the source it follows from (a provider's policy with its section, a standard with its path) or Martin's decision with its date. A requirement without a stated reason binds no programme: look the reason up, then reword the line or drop it.

## What is built first

Describe the first useful part and its order within the horizon.

## What is not built

State the exclusions explicitly.

## The strangest thing the wording permits

Name the nearest wrong reading and distinguish it from the intended scope.

## How work starts from a horizon

A horizon starts no work by itself. Work starts from it as a programme written with Martin in a session; he sets it ready there, and nothing is cut or built before. The basis's not-decided entries are settled in that document under Decided, never when goals are cut.

## Changing the wording

The wording of an open horizon is changed by editing `playback.md` in
`~/mc/martin/horizons/<slug>/` directly, keeping the five headings. It is a file, and a file is edited: no verb exists for it and
none is to be proposed. Never drop a horizon and set it again to change a
sentence; the programmes that name its slug would be left naming a dropped one.
