  all evening on a numbered choice — *Enter to select · ↑/↓ to navigate* —

  1. … 2. …* in red under the area, one capture per running area. A pane
  with neither box nor menu still reads *could not find its prompt*; the two
  states have two different actions and now two different sentences.
