section: Changed

- **A superseded line of Martin's is corrected, not brought to him.** The manager may now correct a dated line of Martin's that a later dated decision on the same page supersedes, keeping its citation, and `mc publish` refuses every other change to a dated line or to `## Decisions`. The canon no longer treats a superseded line, a gate listed under "Martin's" or a requirement that states no reason as his text, so none of them reaches him.
