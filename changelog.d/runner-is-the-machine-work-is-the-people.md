section: Changed

- **NOW is RUNNER, and the sessions have sections of their own.** NOW drew two
  different kinds of thing as one list of dots: the runner's steps, and the
  sessions a person had open. They answer different questions and are stopped
  by different things, and together they meant a `mc plan` left open since
  Sunday sat in the same column as a step four minutes into its budget.
  **RUNNER** is the machine — the steps in flight, one per lane, a pending
  STOP, the stale lane files, and the day behind them. **HELPER** and **BRIEF**
  are one row each, drawn whether or not anybody is at them, because there is
  exactly one of each and *"is the helper running?"* is a question an empty row
  answers as well as a full one. **WORK** is everything else running that the
  runner did not start.
- **Every session says how long it has been open.** `~/mc/runner/foreground/`
  has written `started` since it existed and the page never read it, so on
  2026-09-02 seven live sessions — the oldest three days old, all still holding
  a `claude` process — read as a busy afternoon. WORK is ordered oldest first,
  because the one open longest is the one most likely to have been forgotten,
  and past a day the age turns yellow: by then it is the thing on the row.
- `--json` follows the page, as it must: `now` is now `runner` and `sessions`.
  Inside `runner`, `nowBlock`'s `runner` — the process, not the section — is
  carried as `process`, because `runner.runner.alive` is a sentence nobody
  should have to read. `nowBlock` itself is unchanged; `mc status` shares it.
