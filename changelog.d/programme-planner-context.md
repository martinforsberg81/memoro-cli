section: Added

- **A programme's planner context carries its whole shape, and its queue is visible.** The planner context now lists the phases with their purposes and closure references and the autonomy stop condition beside the brief, every goal, its follow-ups, the events the lease claimed and unreconciled operations; an oversize context still fails explicitly rather than dropping a constraint or goal. `mc programme status` shows pending events with their age and next actor whether autonomy is on or off, and each phase row counts premerge criteria reported. Before this, the queue was visible only as a count, with no age and no owner. Event observation time is recorded once and is not part of an event's identity. Installed provider support is still not claimed: no planner session launches.
