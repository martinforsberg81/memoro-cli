section: Changed

- **The manager pipeline is small boxes, one per stage.** The six full-width
  frames are now a box each, its stage name set into the top border, its count
  and marks centred, joined by arrows under the hero frame and as wide as their
  content only. All six sit on one row when the page has room, else with a
  narrower arrow, else three and three. A box shows at most eight marks of one
  kind, then `●×n`. Its border carries the stage's state (red blocked, green
  worked, yellow waiting, grey empty). Every plan session, interactive or
  headless, is a mark on draft.
