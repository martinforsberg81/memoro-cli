section: Changed

- **The plan session runs once.** The programme planning turn cuts every part
  of a ready programme into a goal, writes its stand on every rule area and
  moves it to build; a goal that waits for another is held by its phase. The
  runner starts no session on a draft: next-draft planning is removed. A turn
  that leaves a draft counts as failed and runs again. The build session
  establishes the acceptance setup in its own worktree before it builds.
