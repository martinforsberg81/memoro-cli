section: Changed

- **`mc deploy` runs the suites in its own worktree, at the pick, and never touches the shared checkout there.** Where main is held in mc's own deploy worktree (`~/.memoro/mc/deploy/memoro`), the gate's suites were served from the shared checkout `~/mc/self/memoro`, which the deploy had to move, wait for and hold, while the script then built in another tree. The suites are now served in the deploy worktree, placed at the pick by the same function the deployer places with (fast-forward when behind, `reset --keep` when past the pick on `origin/main`'s line, untracked strays removed), and the script builds in that tree at that commit. A tree that cannot be placed — modified tracked files, commits `origin/main` lacks, a failed git reading — makes the suites cannot-run naming what stood. The servers take dev-pool slots and are stopped after the exercise; ones already running are left. `mc deploy --dry-run` with no main worktree now makes mc's own and serves the suites in it. Where main is in the person's own checkout, nothing changes.
- **One holder of the deploy worktree at a time.** The exercise, the deployer and `mc language` take a holder record beside the worktree before they place or read it, and wait for a live holder (a line each minute, up to 90 minutes). After that the suites are cannot-run naming the holder, or the deploy's row is `failed` with `stopped_at held`. A holder whose process is gone is taken over.
