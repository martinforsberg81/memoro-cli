- **A programme is designed before its goals are written.**
  `canon/tasks/designing-a-programme.md` says what a programme consists of
  (its README after `canon/tasks/references/programme-readme.md`, goals,
  proposals, rulings in the repository, evidence; nothing else), and the
  order of designing one: aim, reading, every product and policy decision
  collected and put to Martin in one sitting and written into the rulings or
  `docs/technical/`, then the cut into goals, then phases. Goals that can
  run side by side share a phase; a goal that needs another's result or
  edits the same files is in the phase after it. A finished goal stands in
  `build` in its phase from the start; `draft` is a brief still being
  written, not a parking place (Martin, 2026-11-08: too many questions were
  reaching him mid-build at the wrong level, and finished goals waited in
  `draft` for a person to open them). `writing-a-goal.md`, the plan and
  manager roles and `SKILL.md` point at it; the README template that lived
  only under `~/mc/programs/mc/` is now installed with the skill.
