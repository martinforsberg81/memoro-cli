section: Changed

- **`mc plan <programme> --archive` moves the programme's directory to
  `~/mc/archive/programs/`.** It used to release the planning session's
  worktrees and leave `~/mc/programs/<programme>/` in place, so every closed
  programme stayed beside the live ones. Its projects and documents now move
  with it, to `<programme>-2`, `-3`, … when the name was archived before
  (ruling 57).
