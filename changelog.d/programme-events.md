section: Changed

- **Foreground and headless planning share one durable journal and event queue.** `mc goal new|send|revise|criterion` and `mc programme phase|metadata` now run prepare, apply and receipt, so a repeated or crashed command replays instead of changing a goal twice, and a refusal says `contention:` when the cause is another writer. `mc programme events list|ack` shows and consumes events by ID with their evidence reference, with autonomy on or off: attempts, reviews, landings, dependency releases, red main, follow-up results and closed phases. An ack names what the planner observed and refuses unresolved operations; finishing a session no longer acknowledges anything. `mc goal diagnose`, `mc goal retry`, `mc programme wait` and `mc programme operation cancel` are new ordinary verbs.
