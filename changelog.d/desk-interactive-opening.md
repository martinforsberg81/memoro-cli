section: Added

- **`mc martin` opens on its opening.** The desk starts from a model-free
  opening built after a sync of the desk items: what waits for Martin
  (blocking work first, with briefs as written), what was decided without him
  since the last opening, what is not ready, and what the desk cost. The time
  of the last successful opening is kept in `~/mc/martin/last-open.json`.
  `mc martin brief [--json]` prints the opening without starting a session.
  `mc martin get` is unchanged.
