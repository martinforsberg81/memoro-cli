section: Changed

- **Goals live under their programme, by phase.** The page shows criteria,
  sessions run and current status without a separate NEXT or AUTONOMY block.
  Programme rows count open plan sessions per provider; project rows keep the
  number, state, steps and PR. RUNNER carries queue counts, including sent
  goals. Full JSON and programme status stay available. Under a total machine
  cap, lanes take slots first come, first served; `runner/waiting.json` records
  the waiters. Project selection order and uncapped lanes are unchanged.
