section: Added

- **The desk holds horizons, each with its playback.** `mc martin horizon set`
  stores what Martin has decided is worked toward, with its title, its place in
  the order and a five-part playback, under `~/mc/martin/horizons/<slug>/`; the
  order of the open ones is the priority. `horizon show`, `move`, `reached`,
  `reopen` and `drop` and the read-only `horizons [--state] [--json]` complete
  the verbs. Only the interactive desk or a person sets, moves, reopens or
  drops one; the unattended manager may mark one reached. The opening gains a
  Horizons part, shown only when the desk holds one: the horizons reached since
  the last opening with the first lines of what is now true, then the open ones
  in order. A desk with no horizon opens as before.
