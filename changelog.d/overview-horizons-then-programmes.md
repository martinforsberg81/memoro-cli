section: Changed

- **The overview is HORIZONS, then the programmes.** Below HORIZONS `mc` draws every
  programme with something under way as its own frame in name order, headed by its
  number, its horizon (`horizon 5` or `no horizon`) and its phase; the programmes with
  nothing under way are one folded frame, which `a` draws as frames. IN FLIGHT and
  NEEDS ATTENTION are gone: a session is shown under its goal, the desk's sessions are
  counted in DESK's title, held, blocked and malformed goals are shown in their programme,
  and what belongs to no programme is a MAINTENANCE row. The 50-row budget and its folds
  are gone. HORIZONS draws one row per open horizon with its programmes and goals.
