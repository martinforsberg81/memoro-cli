section: Changed

- **What Martin says at the desk is placed there.** A thing said at `mc martin`
  that answers no waiting item is read against the texts, said back in Swedish
  with where it lands, and filed as a proposal, a `mc martin propose` text
  change or a horizon. The opening gains a last part, *Where a new thing goes*
  (the date, the proposal file name shape, the two destinations and the
  programmes), and `brief --json` a `placing` key. Bare `mc helper` is refused
  with a pointer to `mc martin`; the helper role and its entrance are gone.
  `mc helper --collect` and `--intake` are unchanged.
