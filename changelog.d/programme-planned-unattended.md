section: Added

- **A programme is planned into phases and goals with nobody present.** A
  programme with a valid open record and a README page and no goals gets one
  headless planning turn from the runner: it creates every goal as a draft,
  completes, plan-reviews and moves each to build in its phase, and states
  dependencies with `mc programs after`. Its read checkouts of the served
  repositories are separate from `plan/<programme>`, and no next-draft session
  starts while it runs. A turn that leaves no goal counts as a failure; the
  second opens a desk item of kind `programme` (prepared first, then a brief
  for Martin) that closes when the programme holds a goal. Proposal and daily
  turns stay disabled.
