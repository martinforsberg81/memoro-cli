section: Changed

- **Sessions apply decided standards and ask Martin only three kinds of
  question.** The canon now says where decisions live. memoro's
  `docs/technical/` holds the standards for the whole app, a programme's
  rulings hold only its own, and a goal narrows both but never contradicts
  them. A missing standard is decided once and written down, not inside a
  single goal. asking-martin names what reaches Martin: a reserved act, a
  product or policy choice no standard answers, or a bounded exception. Plan
  may, without asking, repair what blocks its own acceptance, fill a technical
  gap, route a SETTLED red to its owner and amend the goal's text after a
  decision. Build and review hold to the standards, and a test of code that
  reads disk reads the same set through that code. Before this, most of
  Martin's goal questions were about budgets, scope exceptions and
  conflicting test rules.
